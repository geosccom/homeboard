Family Dashboard

A private, self-hosted dashboard for one household: a kitchen wall screen and phone app showing the family calendar, shopping lists, weather, budget and family photos.

What it does with Google

The household’s own Google account grants the dashboard read-only access to its Google Drive, so the wall can show the family’s photos as a slideshow and open their own documents. Nothing is modified, deleted, shared or uploaded.

Who can use it

Only the members of the household that runs it. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
